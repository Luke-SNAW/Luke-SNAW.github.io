## [Yuval Noah Harari discusses about AI](https://youtu.be/f84W0KtxDls)

### The Evolution of Intelligence and Language

Yuval Noah Harari discusses the profound societal shifts driven by the rapid development of **Artificial Intelligence**. Historically, human civilization has been held together by language—a shared operating system facilitating laws, religions, and economic structures. Unlike previous historical eras, society is now confronting a technology that is not merely a tool, but an autonomous **agent** capable of making decisions, manipulating language, and potentially controlling the bureaucratic systems that govern human life.

### The Erosion of Truth and Power Dynamics

Democracy relies on human conversation, yet **AI** is increasingly dominating the information landscape by controlling feeds and content. A global arms race is centralizing immense power, as the infrastructure of modern life becomes increasingly dependent on **AI** systems that hold dangerous "kill switches" within imperial hubs.

### The Urgent Need for Wisdom

Currently, the investment ratio between developing more powerful **AI** and ensuring its safety is alarmingly imbalanced, with far less funding allocated to safety. Ultimately, the future hinges on whether humanity can cultivate the wisdom to guide these powerful tools responsibly, rather than becoming subjects of the very systems created to serve us.
